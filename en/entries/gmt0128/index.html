Specification of Datagram Transport Layer Cryptography Protocol

The text and catalog metadata passed current review.

PrinciplesWhy it mattersApplicationsLimits & attacksHistoryWorked example & verificationReferences

Principles

Standard: Specification of Datagram Transport Layer Cryptography Protocol (数据报传输层密码协议规范). GM/T 0128-2023 defines DTLCP: it adapts the TLCP record layer, handshake family and key calculation to UDP loss, reordering, duplication and amplification attacks. The record header adds epoch and a 48-bit seq_num; handshake messages add message_seq, fragment_offset and fragment_length. Large messages are fragmented to path MTU and reassembled by the receiver.

Why it matters

UDP has no TCP connection state or byte-stream semantics, so TLS record rules cannot be copied directly. Cookies let the server validate the source before spending state; explicit sequence numbers let the receiver make replay decisions without a connection.

Epoch increments on key change, separating “sequence under the old key” from “sequence under the new key” and preventing cross-key replay.

Applications

The handshake starts with ClientHello; the server returns HelloVerifyRequest with a cookie, and the client resends with the cookie. Parameters, certificates, ClientKeyExchange and Finished follow.

Key material derives in three levels: pre-master → master → working keys. Working keys split into client/server encryption, integrity and IV keys, selected by direction at the record layer.

Limits & attacks

A common error is treating DTLCP as “TLCP over UDP”: DTLCP records carry explicit sequence numbers, fragmentation and reassembly, unlike TLS implicit sequence numbers and byte streams.

Cookies stop spoofed sources and amplification, not identity; replay-window size is not a fixed standard value. Certificates, trust chains, revocation and Finished still follow PKI and protocol rules.

History

GM/T 0128-2023 was published on 2023-12-04 and took effect on 2024-06-01; it has 18 pages. The national standards platform records CCS L80 and ICS 35.030, and it references GM/Z 4001 and GB/T 38636-2020.

It fills the UDP datagram gap in the Chinese cryptographic protocol stack: TLCP covers connection-oriented transport, DTLCP covers datagram transport.
