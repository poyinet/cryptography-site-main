Cybersecurity Practice Guides

Article review and catalog-metadata acceptance are recorded separately. The library remains under construction.

Principles

Standard: Cybersecurity Practice Guides (网络安全实践指南系列). The NIST SP 1800 series provides reproducible cybersecurity practice reference architectures, not mandatory regulation. The card models prepare, design, implement and operate with a control-evidence chain; it does not invent case data. The card formalises “Cybersecurity Practice Guides” as four relations: Build = {prepare, design, implement, operate} defines the object, |S| = 4 gives the structure, Control = policy ∧ practice ∧ evidence gives the scale or test, and Maturity = f(coverage, evidence) gives the boundary. The formulas use only registered dates, roles and structures; unregistered biographical details, courses, headcounts and device parameters are not invented.

Why it matters

In the 应用安全工程 reading path, Cybersecurity Practice Guides connects to government, finance contexts; the main risks are implementation.

Applications

  1. Fix the object: Build = {prepare, design, implement, operate}. 2. Fill registered parameters; leave unregistered values blank. 3. Use |S| = 4 and Control = policy ∧ practice ∧ evidence to restate structure, scale or test. 4. Check Maturity = f(coverage, evidence), then return to the registered source.

Use: catalog comparison, teaching recap and cross-entry checks; not a substitute for a standard, biography or implementation audit.

A shared name may belong to an algorithm, standard, event, person or term; this entry is responsible only for its catalogue binding and does not absorb adjacent records. If versions, regions or institutions define the name differently, the differences stay side by side.

Limits & attacks

Failure condition: treating this card as the original document, a full biography or a security proof. Maturity = f(coverage, evidence) is only a model boundary; changing the input year, role or parameter changes the conclusion. Person cards do not invent unregistered biography; algorithm cards do not replace standard constants with symbolic parameters.

History

Registered period: 前沿. Exact dates and institutions follow the source ledger.
