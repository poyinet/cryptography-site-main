GM/T 0054-2018 Cryptography Baseline

Article review and catalog-metadata acceptance are recorded separately. The library remains under construction.

PrinciplesWhy it mattersApplicationsLimits & attacksHistoryWorked example & verificationReferences

Principles

Standard: GM/T 0054-2018 General Requirements for Information System Cryptography Application (GM/T 0054-2018 信息系统密码应用基本要求). GM/T 0054-2018 splits information-system cryptography into four technical layers: physical/environment, network/communication, device/compute and application/data. Each layer states requirements by protection level 1 to 4 and marks them shall/should/may for mandatory, recommended and permitted. Besides the technical layers, the standard separates key management and requires security policy, people, implementation and response. Formally, given system S, level L, layer d and item p, R(L,d,p) is shall/should/may; Pass(S,L) requires every shall item to hold, and Overall(S) is the highest level that passes.

Why it matters

This 2018 industry standard was the early baseline for cryptography assessment pilots: it answers “how should systems at different levels use cryptography”, while GM/T 0115 answers “how to assess it”. Merging the two turns requirements into a score and erases the difference between the 2018 edition and the 2021 national GB/T 39786.

The shall/should/may semantics also decide conformity: a system cannot offset a missing shall item with strong should items; key management and security management are baseline parts, not appendices.

Applications

For a gap analysis, fix the system level L, then fill R(L,d,p) and current S(d,p) for the four technical layers plus key management, policy, people, implementation and response. Only shall items are mandatory remediation; should items are improvements; may items are context-dependent.

Assessment uses the GM/T 0115 units, objects and quantitative rules; the Pass predicate here is not a scoring formula. When citing the national baseline, use GB/T 39786-2021 and keep the 2018 industry edition as a version coordinate.

Limits & attacks

Treating GM/T 0054 as an assessment standard misuses it: it lacks GM/T 0115’s assessment units, evidence rules and quantitative scoring. Treating should as shall over-complies; missing a shall item fails outright.

The standard’s own status is a version risk: the national standards record shows “abolished”, while the search page and some sales pages still say “current”, and the official 2021 note confirms the content was raised to GB/T 39786-2021. Historical citations must say “GM/T 0054-2018 industry baseline” and must not present it as the only current basis.

History

GM/T 0054-2018 was published and effective on 2018-02-08 by the then NCA, guiding cryptography application during assessment pilots. In 2021 it was refined and raised to GB/T 39786-2021, effective 2021-10-01.

GM/T 0115-2021 and GM/T 0116-2021 then standardized assessment requirements and process; GM/T 0054 therefore remains mainly as the 2018 industry baseline in historical and cross-standard comparisons.

Glossary

Shall
a requirement that must be met for conformity.
Should
a recommendation, preferred but not mandatory.
May
a permission within the standard’s boundary.
Layer
one of the four technical domains physical, network, device and application.
