# Dictionary Attack

## principle

**Attack**: Dictionary Attack (字典攻击). A dictionary attack uses a brute-force technique of successively trying all words in a large, exhaustive list.

## why

It targets passwords or known plaintext phrases using a candidate list.

Restate the chain as input → state → transform → output → criterion. Every step should identify who acts, what changes and what evidence remains. A step without evidence is marked unverified, not reproduced.

## used

A dictionary attack can attack an authentication service or an encryption service.
