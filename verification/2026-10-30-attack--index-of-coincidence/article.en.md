# Index of Coincidence

## principle

**Attack**: Index of Coincidence (重合指数). The index of coincidence is a measure of the relative frequency of letters in a ciphertext sample, used to determine the period t of a polyalphabetic cipher.

## why

The index of coincidence can determine the period as an alternative to Kasiski’s method.

## used

The index of coincidence is used to analyze polyalphabetic ciphers such as Vigenère.
