# Phishing

## principle

**Attack**: Phishing (钓鱼). Phishing is a technique for attempting to acquire sensitive data through a fraudulent solicitation in email or on a website.

## why

The perpetrator masquerades as a legitimate business or reputable person and uses a lure or bait.
