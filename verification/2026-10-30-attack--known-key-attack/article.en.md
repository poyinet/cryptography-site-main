# Known-Key Attack

## principle

**Attack**: Known-Key Attack (已知密钥攻击). In a known-key attack, an adversary obtains some keys used previously and uses that information to determine new keys.

## why

HAC lists the known-key attack as a class of attacks on protocols.

Restate the chain as input → state → transform → output → criterion. Every step should identify who acts, what changes and what evidence remains. A step without evidence is marked unverified, not reproduced.

## used

Known-key attacks are used to analyze key update and protocol key derivation.
