# Preimage Attack

## principle

**Attack**: Preimage Attack (原像攻击). A preimage attack, given a hash value y, looks for an input x such that h(x)=y.

## why

Preimage resistance requires that finding a preimage be infeasible for essentially all specified outputs.

Restate the chain as input → state → transform → output → criterion. Every step should identify who acts, what changes and what evidence remains. A step without evidence is marked unverified, not reproduced.

## used

Preimage attacks are used to assess the one-wayness of a hash function.

## broken

A preimage attack is not the same as a collision attack; collisions are generally easier to find than preimages.
