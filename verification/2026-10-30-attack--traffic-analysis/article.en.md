# Traffic Analysis

## principle

**Attack**: Traffic Analysis (流量分析). Traffic analysis gains knowledge by inference from observable characteristics of a data flow, even when the information itself is not directly available.

## why

Observable characteristics include source and destination identities and locations, presence, amount, frequency and duration.

Restate the chain as input → state → transform → output → criterion. Every step should identify who acts, what changes and what evidence remains. A step without evidence is marked unverified, not reproduced.

## used

Traffic analysis is used to infer information from communication patterns even when the payload is encrypted.
