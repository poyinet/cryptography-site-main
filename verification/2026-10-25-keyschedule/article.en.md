# Key schedules: from a master key to round keys

## principle

**Term**: Key Schedule. It is registered under symmetric block ciphers. The definition gives a superordinate concept, a differentia, a positive example and a counterexample. A synonym or abbreviation is not a definition.

## why

It is listed because it connects concrete records, objects or procedures in symmetric block ciphers, not because of its name.

## used

Fix the object and its aliases; list the sourced dates, roles, parameters or inscriptions; state the observable output; then test the boundary. Any step without a source stops there rather than being filled with prose.

## broken

The boundary separates a sourced fact, a model-derived relation and an unverified gap. Secondary summaries yield to primary material; unsourced dates, counts and device values stay blank.

## history

Registered period: Age of Standardization. The exact date is left to the ledger; institutional placement and technical detail follow the cited sources.
