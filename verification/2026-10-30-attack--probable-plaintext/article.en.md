# Probable Plaintext

## principle

**Attack**: Probable Plaintext (概然明文). A probable-plaintext attack guesses a word or phrase likely present in the plaintext and uses it to recover the key or plaintext.

## why

The attacker subtracts the guessed word from all possible starting locations and looks for a recognizable result.

Restate the chain as input → state → transform → output → criterion. Every step should identify who acts, what changes and what evidence remains. A step without evidence is marked unverified, not reproduced.

## used

Probable-word attacks apply to running-key ciphers and polyalphabetic substitution.

## broken

The attack depends on guessing the right word; a wrong guess or insufficient context fails.
