供应链攻击

本篇详解的复核与目录元数据验收分别记录；全库仍在逐项建设。

原理与机制为什么重要应用与实例边界与攻击面历史与背景逐步示例与核验参考出处

原理与机制

术语：供应链攻击（Supply Chain Attack）。NIST 对风险管理的答案是 §1.5 的定义：「C-SCRM is the organized and purposeful management of cybersecurity risks throughout the supply chain」，§1.2 补充它是「an enterprise-wide activity that should be directed as such from a governance perspective」，并说明「supply chain refers to the linked set of resources and processes between and among multiple levels of an enterprise, each of which is an acquirer」。

为什么重要

范围之所以宽，是因为风险来自链条本身：§1 与 §2.2 把供应链网络安全风险描述为「the potential for harm or compromise that may arise from suppliers, their supply chains, their products, or their services」，§1.5 则把 C-SCRM 的活动划为「activities that span the entire SDLC, including research and development, design, manufacturing, acquisition, delivery, integration, operations and maintenance, disposal」。

应用与实例

方法采用多层风险管理：§2.3 引用 SP 800-39 的「three levels … 1) the enterprise level, 2) the mission and business process level, and 3) the operational level.C-SCRM requires the involvement of all three levels」，各级产出分别是整体策略与计划、中层策略与计划，以及系统级 C-SCRM 计划；§2.3.4 称「the CSCRM plan is a living document」。§1.6 说明本出版物把 SP 800-37 Rev.2 的框架加上 an enhanced overlay of specific C-SCRM controls, building on NIST SP 800-53 Rev.5。

边界与攻击面

风险会沿链条下沉并汇聚：附录 G 要求在关键性分析中包含「critical fourth-party suppliers」，因为「an enterprise may find exposures to cybersecurity risks that result from third-party suppliers receiving critical input or services from a common fourth-party supplier」；控制 SR-3(2) 要求把控制「flow down this requirement to relevant sub-tier contractors throughout the SDLC」。§2.2 列举的实例包括「Insiders working on behalf of a system integrator」。完整性方面的对应做法是「code authentication mechanisms, such as digital signatures」与「SBOMs should be digitally signed using a verifiable and trusted key」。

历史与背景

现行版本是 NIST SP 800-161r1-upd1，封面标注「May 2022 INCLUDES UPDATES AS OF 11-01-2024; SEE APPENDIX K」，发布历史记录经 NIST 编辑评审委员会于 2024 年 9 月 25 日批准，并明确「Supersedes NIST Special Publication 800-161r1 (May 2022)」。作者团队包括 Boyens、Smith、Bartol、Winkler、Holbrook 与 Fallon。
